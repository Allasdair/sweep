Privacy

Sweep · no-guess minesweeper · Short version: Sweep collects nothing.

  • No accounts, ads, analytics or trackers. Nothing about you or how you play is sent anywhere.
  • Your progress stays on your device, in your browser's storage. Clearing site data removes it, so keep a backup (Settings → Backup & restore).
  • No network requests beyond loading the game's own files from this site. Fonts are bundled, and a Content Security Policy blocks connections to any other site.
  • Links you share (challenge codes and league codes) contain only what the game shows: the board, your times, the name you chose and a random player ID. They go only where you send them.
  • Backups are a code or a text file that you keep. Sweep never uploads them.
  • Open source under the MIT licence: github.com/Allasdair/sweep.
